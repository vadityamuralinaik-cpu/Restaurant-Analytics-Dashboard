# Restaurant Analytics & Customer Insights Dashboard

An interactive data analytics project built using **MySQL, SQL, Power BI, and DAX** to analyze restaurant performance, customer preferences, pricing, demographics, and ratings.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Relational Data Modeling

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The project contains 5 relational tables:

- **138 Consumers**
- **56 Restaurants**
- **330 Consumer Preferences**
- **45 Restaurant Cuisine Records**
- **647 Ratings**

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Consumer Overview
- Consumer distribution by city
- Average age by city
- Consumer budget distribution
- Consumer age groups

### 2. Restaurant Analysis
- Restaurant cuisine distribution
- Food and service ratings
- Overall rating distribution
- Restaurant pricing
- Alcohol service and smoking availability

### 3. Consumer Preferences
- Most preferred cuisines
- Consumer budget categories
- Average age by city
- Consumer age groups

### 4. Restaurant Dashboard
- Total Ratings
- Average Overall Rating
- Average Food Rating
- Average Service Rating

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Mexican cuisine was the most preferred cuisine among consumers.
- Medium-priced restaurants represented the largest restaurant price category.
- Restaurant ratings were analyzed across different price categories.
- Food, service, and overall ratings were compared to understand restaurant performance.
- Consumer demographics and preferences were analyzed to identify customer patterns.
